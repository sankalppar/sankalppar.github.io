I am a research assistant at IIT Bombay, working with Prof. Sunita Sarawagi and Prof. Preethi Jyothi on making speech recognition more reliable. I also work with Prof. Srikanth Raj Chetupalli on speech recognition for regionally accented Hindi conversations in rural primary healthcare. I am broadly interested in speech and language processing, especially for accented speech and low-resource languages.

Previously, I was a quantitative strategist at Quadeye, a high-frequency trading firm. I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay, where I worked with Prof. Preethi Jyothi on speech recognition for code-switched speech.
